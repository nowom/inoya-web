Privacy Policy — INOYA

Last updated: June 11, 2026

1. Introduction

INOYA respects your privacy and is designed to support reflection, emotional reset, and personal clarity with minimal and purposeful data collection.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when using the INOYA mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use INOYA, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect your email address, authentication identifiers, and basic account settings. If you continue as a guest, some data may be stored only locally or linked to a temporary guest session.

User Content

INOYA allows you to create personal reflections, emotional check-ins, priorities, small next steps, and saved moments of clarity as part of the guided reset experience.

Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash reports, and basic diagnostic information needed to maintain and improve the app.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we may store notification preferences and reminder schedules, such as reminders for a daily mood check or your next small step.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • provide and maintain the app;
  • save your reflections and moments of clarity;
  • allow you to review your reset history;
  • support account login and authentication;
  • send reminders and notifications if you enable them;
  • improve app stability, performance, and reliability;
  • respond to support requests;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Reflections and User Content

Your reflections, notes, emotional inputs, priorities, and small steps are stored only to provide the core functionality of INOYA and to allow you to return to your saved moments of clarity.

You retain ownership of the content you create inside the app. You are responsible for the content you enter.

5. Data Storage

Depending on the app configuration and how you use it, your data may be stored locally on your device and/or synced with backend services used to operate the app.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security or permanent availability of your data.

6. Third-Party Services

INOYA may use third-party services required to operate the application, including services for authentication, data storage, notifications, diagnostics, and application distribution.

Examples may include:

  • Supabase;
  • Google Play Services;
  • Apple App Store Services.

These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide the app, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. Some information may be retained if required by law or for legitimate security, fraud prevention, or operational reasons.

8. Guest Use

If you use INOYA as a guest, some data may remain stored only on your device or may be connected to a temporary session. Guest data may be lost if you delete the app, change devices, clear local storage, or reset the application.

9. Children's Privacy

INOYA is not intended for children under the age required by applicable laws in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we learn that a child has provided personal data, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.
